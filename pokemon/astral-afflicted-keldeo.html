Astral Afflicted Keldeo

The Colt Pokémon

Pokédex Entry

Unable to fully absorb Astral energy, this proud creature succumbed to Astral Affliction. Flooded with far more raw energy from the cosmos than its body can safely withstand, it now wanders and stumbles through the wilderness of Monsai in violent torment, throwing off agonizing ripples of psychic pressure that warp the air around it.
