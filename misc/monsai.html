Monsai

Author: Ozzy May 17, 2026

The Monsai region is one of our untamed regions, called a frontier! It is heavily inspired by the wide-open landscapes of Montana and the spirit of the American Wild West, Monsai offers a bold departure from traditional regions. Here, there is no established League, only wilderness, mystery, and the thrill of forging your own path. Exploration is at the heart of Monsai’s identity. As a newly charted region, it’s a land of opportunity and risk, where many trainers try to make their mark. Every mountain, canyon trail, and ruin hold the promise of discovery (whether it’s a new species of Pokémon, a buried legend, or a secret long lost to time). Technology across the monsian settlements is very minimal and rustic, with survival depending more on grit, bonds with Pokémon, and clever use of the land than on fancy gadgets or global systems. Each outpost or homestead you encounter tells a story of settlers before and of the wild creatures that share the frontier. Whether looking to chart the unknown, round up wild Pokémon, or chase legends, the Monsai region is yours to explore. No two journeys are the same because in Monsai, the path isn't given... it's made.
