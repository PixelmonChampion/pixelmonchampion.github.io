Sheriff Rowans

The Law of Monsai
Author: Ozzy
May 25, 2026

The stoic moral compass of the region who values order and "the letter of the law" above personal feelings. They have a reputation for being unshakeable in the face of danger. They love patrolling the dustrail train tracks, arbitrating disputes between trainers, and practicing their Quick ball techniques. Their partner Pokémon is Bibivire.

Important Note: It is said the sheriff has a sketchy side, leading some townspeople to believe he has an active deal with the Bandit Brigade. Observers question how he uniquely possesses standard factory-line Poké Balls when everyone else is restricted to hand-crafted ones...
