PokéFrontiers Open Beta Launch

Author: Ozzy Aug 24, 2026

The PokéFrontiers v1.0 Full Release is officially here, and we're so excited to finally show what we have been working on to everyone! Not only is the wiki fully updated, but you can jump in right now and continue your journey through Monsai. There is a ton to discover, find, and craft in this update. We are also hard at work shifting everything to Cobblemon 1.8 and developing our first major title updates, v1.1 and v1.2. Be sure to check out our teaser roadmap trailer below for a sneak peek! And one of our final things we want to mention is our first upcoming doubles tourney (in the Monsai National format) coming very soon! Don't forget to join our Discord to check out the v1.0 changelog and learn how to support the server as an Investor!
